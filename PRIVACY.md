# Privacy Policy — Pause&PlayHere

Pause&PlayHere is an offline-first, open-source media player.

- **Data Collection:** We collect zero personal data, zero telemetry, zero crash analytics, and zero usage tracking.
- **Local Files:** Media and subtitle files you open remain strictly on your device.
- **Network Streams:** When you manually enter a stream URL, the app connects directly to that URL without any intermediary server.
